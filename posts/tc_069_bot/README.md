# Turning your setup into a bot? Know these rules first.

Thanks for asking. Here's how we work. We build YOUR strategy, not ours:

1. You share your setup: instrument, timeframe, entry, exit, stop loss, position size.
2. We test it first on years of real data with real costs, and give you the honest result, even if it says "don't automate this".
3. We code it as your own algo: you see every rule, you own the code, it runs from your machine or static IP through your broker's API, using limit orders.
4. Paper trading for at least 8 weeks, comparing the bot's trades with what the rules say.
5. Then you decide whether to go live. We stay on for maintenance if you want.

Pricing: the test alone (Strategy Truth Test) is ₹4,999 per strategy, report in 3 working days. A custom bot starts at ₹30,000 and includes the test. Here's a sample report and the request form: tradecopilot.in/strategy-test

We don't sell strategies, tips or "guaranteed return" bots. Reply with your setup and your broker, or send it through the form, and we'll confirm the rules and price before anything is charged.
